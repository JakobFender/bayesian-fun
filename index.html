      01 — Coin bench

      Is this coin fair?

      The hypothesis lab handles yes/no questions. Here the unknown is a number, the coin's bias θ = P(heads). Your belief about it is a whole distribution, a Beta(α, β). Each heads adds 1 to α and each tails adds 1 to β. That is the entire update rule.

      02 — Distribution

      Where the bias probably is

      The dashed curve is where you started; the solid curve is where you are now. The shaded band is the 95% credible interval. Watch it narrow as flips accumulate. Doubt shrinks roughly with 1/√n, which is slower than anyone would like.
