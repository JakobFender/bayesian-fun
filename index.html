    The coin bench is this coin fair?

    Hypothesis lab handles yes/no questions. Here the unknown is a number, the coin's bias θ = P(heads). Your belief about it is a whole distribution: a Beta(α, β). Each heads adds 1 to α and each tails adds 1 to β. That's the whole update rule.

    A mystery coin with an unknown bias is on the bench. Flip it and see how fast you work it out.
